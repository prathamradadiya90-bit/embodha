Pricing

Every price is on the course page, and on the button, before you pay. The price you see is the price you pay: there's nothing to ship, so no delivery charge, and no fees on top.

Courses

Each course has its own price, which you pay once. It stays in your library for as long as Embodha runs, on every device you sign in to. One course is free to everyone, and many courses have a free chapter to try first, so you know the teacher's voice before you pay.

Following a teacher

Some teachers offer a monthly subscription at a price they set. It opens every course they make, including new ones, and renews every month until you stop it. See the terms for how to stop it.

Currency and tax

  • In India, prices are in rupees and include 18% GST. Your invoice shows the tax.
  • Elsewhere, prices are in US dollars.
  • In the iOS and Android apps, the App Store and Google Play show the price in your own currency, as close to ours as their price lists allow, and take the payment themselves.

Paying on the website

Payments on the website go through Razorpay, by UPI, card or the other ways its checkout offers. We never see your card or bank details. You get an invoice by email straight after.
