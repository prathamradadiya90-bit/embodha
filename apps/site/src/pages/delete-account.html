Delete your account

You can delete your Embodha account at any time, from the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Embodha and go to You.
  2. Tap Delete account at the bottom, and confirm.

Without the app

Email {{supportEmail}} from the email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We'll reply to confirm it's done.

What happens

  • You're signed out on every device straight away.
  • Subscriptions bought on the website or on Google Play stop renewing, and the month you've paid for runs out as normal.
  • Subscriptions bought in the App Store can only be stopped by you: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  • Thirty days later your account is erased: your profile, practice history, streak, notes, reviews, library and notification history. If you sign in again before then, the deletion is cancelled and everything is as you left it.

What we keep

  • Records of payments and invoices, because tax law requires it (in India, at least six years). They're kept against an empty account with no email, name or other details.
  • Records of how the app was used, with nothing linking them to you.
