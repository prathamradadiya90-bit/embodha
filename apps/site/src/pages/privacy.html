What we collect

Your account

Your email address, which is how you sign in: we send a six-digit code or a one-tap link, and there's no password to remember. After you join we ask for your name, your country and the year you were born, which is how we check you're 18 or over. In India we also ask for your state, because it decides how GST is split on website invoices. Gender is up to you. We also keep your time zone, the goals you picked, your reminder time and your settings.

When you sign in we note which devices are signed in to your account, and the internet (IP) address a sign-in request came from, to stop people guessing codes.

Your practice

What you play and for how long (the app checks in every 30 seconds while something plays, on the phone or on a TV it has sent the chapter to), where you stopped, which chapters you've finished, your breathing sessions and your streak. Notes you write on a chapter are yours alone, and we store them encrypted. Reviews you post are public in the app, with your first name and the first letter of your last name. We keep what you search for, to improve search.

Downloads

If you download chapters to play without a connection, we keep a list of the phones your downloads are on (each phone's model, like "Pixel 8", and when it was last online) and which chapters are on each. That's how downloads stay on three phones at most, and come off a phone when a course stops being yours. The files themselves stay on your phone, and what you play offline reaches us when the phone is next online, like any other practice.

Purchases

What you bought, when, the price, the tax and the invoice number, and the payment's reference from Razorpay, the App Store or Google Play. Card, UPI and bank details go to those companies directly and never reach us.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, an address for your phone that lets us send them, which of our messages you've read, and when your daily reminder was sent.

If you teach on Embodha

Your teacher profile, your courses and uploads, your earnings and payouts, and the bank and tax details we need to pay you.

When you write to us

Your email and anything you send with it. Feedback sent from the app also carries the app version, your phone's model and system version, and the screen you were on, so we can look into what you saw. It reaches the team by email as well, so they can reply.

How the app is used

The app tells our own server about a few moments, so we can see whether people find what they came for:

  • opening the app, and which screens you open;
  • opening a course page, or reaching a chapter you don't have yet;
  • starting, finishing or abandoning a purchase;
  • starting a breath;
  • how long a chapter took to start playing, and whether the phone was on Wi-Fi or mobile data (and which kind, like 4G);
  • sending a chapter to a TV with Chromecast, and whether the TV could play it.

Each moment carries the course or screen it was about, never anything you typed. To count visitors who aren't signed in, each install of the app makes up a random number; signing out makes a new one. None of this goes to an outside analytics or advertising company.

If the app crashes or runs into an error, it sends us a report: what went wrong in the code, the screen it happened on, the app version, and your phone's model and system version. Never the name you gave your phone, and nothing you typed. A crash is kept on the phone until the app next opens, then sent.

To stop all of this, switch off Share how you use the app under You. We still need to know what you play, to keep your place and your streak and to pay teachers.

How long we keep it

  • Your account: until you delete it. Deleting signs you out everywhere straight away. Thirty days later the account and everything tied to it is erased: profile, practice, notes, reviews, feedback, library and notification history. If you sign back in during those 30 days, the deletion is cancelled.
  • Payments and invoices: kept for as long as tax law requires (in India, at least six years). If you've bought something, an empty account record stays for those payments to point to, with no email, name or other details.
  • How the app was used, and what was searched for: once your account is erased, those records stay, but nothing links them to you.
  • Crash and error reports: 90 days, then deleted.
  • Sign-in codes: stop working after 10 minutes.
  • Backups: kept for {{backupDays}} days, then overwritten.

Your choices and rights

  • See it. Under You, Your data emails you a file of everything we hold about you.
  • Correct it. Under You, Your details. For anything else, like your email address, write to us and we'll put it right.
  • Delete it. Under You, Delete account. Or see deleting your account for other ways.
  • Switch things off: notifications in your phone's settings, reminders and usage sharing under You.
  • Name someone to act for you if you die or can't act yourself, as India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act allows. Write to us.
  • Complain. Write to our Grievance Officer (below). We acknowledge complaints within 48 hours and answer within a month. If you're not satisfied, you can go to the Data Protection Board of India. If you live in the European Union or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority.

Keeping it safe

Everything travels encrypted. Notes are encrypted when stored. We keep sign-in codes and session keys only as fingerprints that can't be turned back into the real thing. Our team's access depends on their role, every change they make is recorded, and their sessions end after 12 hours idle. No system is perfect, and if something goes wrong that affects you, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Embodha is for adults. If you're under 18, please don't use it. If we learn a child has an account, we delete it.
