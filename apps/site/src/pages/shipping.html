Shipping and delivery

In effect from {{effectiveDate}}

Nothing on Embodha is shipped. Courses and subscriptions are digital, and they reach your account as soon as your payment is confirmed. There are no shipping or delivery charges.

When it arrives

Usually within seconds of paying. The course, or every course from the teacher you've subscribed to, appears in Library and unlocks on its course page, on every device where you're signed in to the same account.

Now and then a payment takes longer, when your bank, the App Store or Google Play marks it as pending. It unlocks as soon as it clears.

If it hasn't arrived

In the iOS or Android app, go to You and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the account you bought it with.

Still not there after 15 minutes? Write to {{supportEmail}} with the receipt or the payment reference, and we'll put it right or refund you.
